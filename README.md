# SC Food Startup Kit

A practical open-source toolkit for:

- Food Trucks
- Food Trailers
- Mobile Vendors
- Caterers
- Festival Vendors
- Pop-Up Food Operations
- Plate Sale Entrepreneurs

Built to help South Carolina food businesses launch safely,
legally, and profitably.

## Included

✅ Startup Guides

✅ Permit Checklists

✅ Inspection Preparation

✅ Food Safety Logs

✅ Catering Templates

✅ Event Planning Forms

✅ Budget Worksheets

✅ Operations Binder

## Disclaimer

This repository is an educational resource and is not legal advice.

Always verify current requirements with:

- South Carolina Department of Agriculture (SCDA)
- South Carolina Department of Revenue
- Local City Licensing Offices
- Local Fire Marshal

## Mission

Help small food entrepreneurs succeed while protecting food safety and public health.
