# SC Food Startup Kit

An open-source toolkit for food trucks, food trailers, caterers, concession vendors, and plate-sale entrepreneurs.

## Mission

Help food businesses launch legally, operate safely, and grow sustainably.

## Repository Contents

### Guides
- Startup Guide
- Menu Engineering
- Food Safety
- Catering Operations
- Event Planning
- Festival Vendor Resources

### Templates
- Temperature Logs
- Cleaning Logs
- Catering Contracts
- Inspection Checklists
- Incident Reports

### Business Resources
- Menu Costing
- Break-Even Analysis
- Startup Budgeting
- Profit Planning

## Disclaimer

This repository is an educational resource and is not legal, tax, health, or regulatory advice.

Always verify requirements with:

- South Carolina Department of Agriculture (SCDA)
- South Carolina Department of Revenue (SCDOR)
- Local municipalities
- Local fire authorities
