# Food Safety Fundamentals
*SC Food Startup Kit*

## Why Food Safety Matters
Protect customers, employees, and your business.

## Major Risk Areas
### Improper Temperatures
- Cold holding: 41°F or below
- Hot holding: 135°F or above

### Inadequate Cooking
- Poultry: 165°F
- Ground meats: 155°F
- Seafood: 145°F

### Cross Contamination
Separate raw and ready-to-eat foods.

### Unsafe Sources
Purchase from approved suppliers.

### Personal Hygiene
Wash hands frequently and stay home when sick.

## Inspection Readiness
Maintain:
- Permits
- Temperature logs
- Cleaning logs
- Training records
