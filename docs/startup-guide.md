# Startup Guide
*SC Food Startup Kit*

## Introduction
Starting a food truck, food trailer, catering business, or plate-sale operation requires planning, licensing, food safety, and financial discipline.

## Phase 1: Define Your Concept
- What will you sell?
- Who is your customer?
- What makes you unique?

## Phase 2: Choose a Business Structure
- Sole Proprietorship
- LLC
- Corporation

## Phase 3: Register Your Business
- EIN
- Business bank account
- Accounting system

## Phase 4: Obtain Permits
- Food permit
- Sales tax registration
- Local licenses
- Fire approvals

## Phase 5: Build Your Menu
Focus on profitable, repeatable, fast-selling items.

## Phase 6: Create Systems
- Opening checklist
- Closing checklist
- Cleaning program
- Temperature logs

## Phase 7: Soft Launch
Test operations before major events.

## Startup Success Formula
Simple Menu + Strong Systems + Food Safety = Sustainable Business
