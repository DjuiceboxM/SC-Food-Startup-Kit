# Startup Budget Guide
*SC Food Startup Kit - Business Series*

---

# Why Startup Budgeting Matters

One of the biggest mistakes new food entrepreneurs make is underestimating startup costs.

A realistic budget helps you:

- Avoid cash shortages
- Plan funding needs
- Reduce financial stress
- Launch with confidence

---

# The Startup Capital Formula

```text
Startup Capital Needed =
Startup Costs
+
Operating Reserve
+
Emergency Fund
```

Never launch with only enough money to open.

You also need enough money to survive.

# Emergency Fund

Plan for unexpected events:

Examples:

- Generator failure
- Refrigerator failure
- Vehicle repairs
- Slow sales periods

Recommended:

```text
10% - 20%
of startup costs
```

---

# Break-Even Estimate

Formula:

```text
Monthly Expenses ÷ Average Profit Per Sale
```

Example:

```text
Monthly Expenses = $5,000

Average Profit Per Sale = $10

Break-Even Sales Needed:

500 Sales Per Month
```

# Financial Warning Signs

Avoid launching if:

- No emergency fund exists
- No operating reserve exists
- Pricing is based on competitors alone
- Startup costs are being guessed
