# Permits and Licenses
*SC Food Startup Kit*

## Overview
Food businesses typically require approvals from multiple agencies.

## Business Registration
Maintain copies of:
- Formation documents
- EIN confirmation

## Sales Tax Registration
Register and maintain filing records.

## Food Permit
Keep:
- Food permit
- Inspection reports
- Corrective action records

## Local Licensing
May include:
- City licenses
- County licenses
- Vendor permits

## Fire Requirements
Maintain inspection and suppression-system records.

## Insurance
- General liability
- Commercial auto
- Property coverage
- Workers compensation (if applicable)
