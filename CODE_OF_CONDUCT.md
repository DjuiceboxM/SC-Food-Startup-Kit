# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and professional environment for everyone participating in the SC Food Startup Kit project.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Sharing knowledge generously
- Providing evidence-based information
- Helping newcomers learn
- Accepting feedback professionally

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Trolling or inflammatory comments
- Knowingly sharing unsafe food-handling advice
- Spam, scams, or unsolicited advertising

## Food Safety First

Because this project focuses on food businesses, contributors should prioritize public health and food safety. Content that encourages unsafe practices may be removed.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject content that violates this Code of Conduct.
