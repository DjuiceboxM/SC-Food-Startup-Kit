# Contributing to SC Food Startup Kit

First, thank you for contributing.

This project exists to help food entrepreneurs, caterers, food truck operators, concession vendors, church organizations, nonprofit fundraisers, and small business owners navigate the world of mobile food operations.

Whether you're a health inspector, restaurant owner, food truck operator, accountant, chef, event coordinator, attorney, or someone who learned lessons the hard way, your experience can help someone else succeed.

---

# Our Mission

To build an open-source knowledge base that helps food businesses:

- Launch legally
- Operate safely
- Remain compliant
- Become profitable
- Serve their communities

We believe that good information should be accessible.

# Before Submitting

Please ensure your contribution is:

## Accurate

Provide information that is factual and verifiable.

---

## Current

Regulations change regularly.

Include dates when referencing:

- Laws
- Regulations
- Permit requirements

---

## Practical

Favor real-world solutions over theory.

Food operators need resources they can use immediately.

---

## Professional

Keep language respectful and helpful.

---

# Style Guide

## File Names

Use lowercase and hyphens.

Good:

```text
food-safety.md
menu-engineering.md
startup-budget.md
```

Avoid:

```text
FoodSafety.MD
MenuEngineeringFinal2.md
```

---

## Headings

Use clear logical hierarchy.

Example:

```markdown
# Main Section

## Subsection

### Supporting Topic
```

---

## Lists

Use bullet points whenever possible.

Example:

```markdown
- Handwashing
- Temperature Control
- Cross Contamination Prevention
```

---

# Markdown Standards

Use fenced code blocks.

Example:

````markdown
```text
Food Cost =
Food Cost ÷ Selling Price × 100
```
````

Do not use screenshots when text or tables can communicate the information better.

---

# Regulatory Information

Whenever regulatory information is added:

## Include Source

Example:

```markdown
Source:
South Carolina Department of Agriculture
```

---

## Include Jurisdiction

Example:

```markdown
Applies To:

- South Carolina
- Mobile Food Establishments
```

---

## Avoid Legal Advice

This project is educational.

Do not present:

- Legal advice
- Tax advice
- Professional engineering advice

Instead:

```text
Consult qualified professionals as needed.
```

# Pull Request Process

## Step 1

Fork repository.

---

## Step 2

Create feature branch.

Example:

```bash
git checkout -b improve-food-cost-guide
```

---

## Step 3

Make changes.

---

## Step 4

Validate:

- Spelling
- Formatting
- Links
- Calculations

---

## Step 5

Commit your work.

Example:

```bash
git commit -m "Improve food cost examples"
```

---

## Step 6

Submit Pull Request.

Include:

- What changed
- Why it changed
- References used
